### AdaPhish: AI-Powered Adaptive Defense and Education Resource Against Deceptive Emails

Here is the analysis of the research paper in a clear structured format:

**RESEARCH QUESTION:**
What problem does it address?
* The paper addresses the problem of effective methods to tackle phishing attacks without leaking sensitive information, and the limitations of traditional phish bowl initiatives.

**METHODOLOGY:**
How did they approach it?
* The authors propose a collaborative platform, AdaPhish, powered by large language models (LLM) and a vector database, to automatically anonymize and analyze phishing emails.
* The platform uses a dual approach with two AI models to enhance detection accuracy, one for comparing emails and detecting new phishing tactics, and the other for assessing risks based on pre-trained knowledge.

**KEY FINDING:**
Main result, in 1-2 sentences
* AdaPhish is an AI-powered phish bowl platform that automatically anonymizes and analyzes phishing emails, achieving real-time detection and adaptation to new phishing tactics, while enabling long-term tracking of phishing trends.

**LIMITATION:**
Any stated weakness or gap, or "Not stated" if none
* Not stated. The paper does not mention any limitations or weaknesses of the proposed platform, AdaPhish.
